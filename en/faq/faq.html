Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does website SEO take?

SEO is a long-term process. Usually, the first effects are visible after 3-6 months, but full results can take up to a year, depending on the competitiveness of the industry and the current state of the website.

2. Can I manage my website's SEO on my own?

Of course, the basics of SEO can be mastered on your own. However, a professional approach and the experience of an agency allow for more effective actions and better results.

3. What information is needed to start cooperation?

To begin, we need access to your website, Google Analytics account, and Google Search Console. Additionally, information about your industry, competition, and business goals is very helpful.

4. Do you guarantee the 1st place in search results?

No one can guarantee a specific spot on Google because search engine algorithms are constantly changing and independent of agencies. However, we guarantee professional actions based on proven strategies that systematically increase visibility and traffic to the site.

5. What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) involves long-term actions aimed at building natural visibility in organic results. Google Ads is paid advertising that gives immediate results, but traffic stops the moment you stop paying for the campaign.

6. Do I have to pay for SEO regularly?

Yes, SEO is a continuous process. Search engines and competitor activities are constantly changing. Regular work on optimization and new content allows you not only to achieve high positions but, most importantly, to maintain them in the long run.

7. Does local SEO differ from national SEO?

Yes, local SEO focuses on users in a specific region (e.g., Częstochowa). Key elements include phrases with the city name and the optimization of your Google Business Profile, which helps reach customers in your immediate vicinity.

8. What is an SEO audit and why is it important?

An SEO audit is a detailed analysis of a website in terms of technical, content, and link aspects. It is crucial because it allows for the detection of errors blocking the site from high rankings and sets a precise strategy for further corrective actions.

9. How important is content for SEO?

Content is the foundation of SEO. Google promotes websites that provide unique and valuable information. Regular publication of substantive articles helps build site authority and allows for positioning on a larger number of keywords.

10. Does page speed affect its ranking?

Yes, page loading speed (PageSpeed) is an official Google ranking factor. Slow pages negatively impact user experience, leading to a higher bounce rate and drops in search results.
